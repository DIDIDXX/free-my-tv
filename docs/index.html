FAQ

        
Good questions.

      

      

        
Is it safe?
It only uses Android's standard developer commands — no root, nothing uninstalled. Picture, sound, inputs, remote and Google Play Services stay untouched, and one button brings back stock.

        
Does it work on Android 11 TCL TVs?
Yes — this fork specifically targets Android 11 compatibility, cuts GPU frame jank from ~50% down to ~1%, and removes stock launcher flicker.

        
Can I disable football or weather?
Yes: pick Disabled for the club or uncheck weather in the configurator above.

        
Does it collect any data?
No accounts, no analytics, no tracking. It only talks directly to ESPN (when a club is enabled), Open-Meteo (when weather is enabled), Binance, and TMDB (if you configure a key).
