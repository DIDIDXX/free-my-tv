What's in this fork

        
Built on top of FreeMyTV, tuned for daily TCL use.

        
Keeps the clean foundation of simbasjungles/free-my-tv while solving Android 11 jank, Home button flicker, and full customization.

      


      

        

          

            01 / NAVIGATION & HOME
            
Full-screen Apps grid + zero TCL flash on Home.

            
Replaces the static Movies tab with a crisp 16:9 Apps tab listing everything installed on your TV. On Android 11, an Accessibility bridge handles the Home button directly so the stock TCL UI doesn't flash in between.

          

          

        


        

          

            02 / LIVE CLUB TAB
            
Auto-syncing football dashboard (20 Série A clubs).

            
Fixes stale fixtures by querying all competitions dynamically, showing live matches with 60s updates, local timezone kickoff times, and one-click YouTube/SmartTube highlights.

          

          

        


        

          

            03 / PERFORMANCE
            
60 fps drawer with weather & live markets.

            
Cached RenderNode layers keep animations smooth on modest TV GPUs. Press down anytime for weather and market prices — or toggle weather off at build time.

FAQ

        
Quick answers.

      

      

        
Do I need to know how to code?
No. Pick your preferences above, copy the tailored prompt, and paste it into Claude Code. It guides you step by step with your TV remote.

        
Can I revert everything anytime?
Yes. Nothing is uninstalled. Disabled stock packages can be re-enabled anytime via the in-app Original button.

        
Can I disable football or weather?
Yes — choose Disabled in step 1 or uncheck weather in step 2 so your APK skips them completely.

        
How does this differ from upstream FreeMyTV?
This fork of simbasjungles/free-my-tv targets TCL Android 11 smoothness, eliminates Home-button launcher flash, replaces Movies with a full Apps grid, supports 20 Série A clubs, and adds this 1-prompt onboarding builder.
